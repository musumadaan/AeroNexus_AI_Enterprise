# NTSB accident data
The NTSB database records accidents and serious incidents with a probable cause statement written by investigators after the investigation.

# ASRS reports
ASRS reports are voluntary, de-identified narratives. NASA does not verify or validate them, so counts are not statistically representative of all events.

# Service difficulty reports
FAA service difficulty reports describe malfunctions and defects reported by operators and repair stations. They are useful for component trend analysis but are not verified failure rates.

# Use of predictions
A severity score from text is for triage and prioritizing reviews; it never replaces an investigation.
