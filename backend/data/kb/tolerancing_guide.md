# General tolerances
The title block general tolerance applies to every dimension without its own tolerance. A general tolerance of plus or minus 0.5 mm is typical for laser cut plate; plus or minus 2 mm is typical for large rolled culvert plate.

# Standards
ASME Y14.5 defines dimensioning and geometric tolerancing practice in North America. ISO 2768-1 defines general tolerances for linear dimensions in ISO-based drawings.

# Cost impact
Tighter tolerances increase cost because they need slower cutting, machining or extra inspection. Flag tolerances tighter than plus or minus 0.25 mm on plate work for estimator review.
