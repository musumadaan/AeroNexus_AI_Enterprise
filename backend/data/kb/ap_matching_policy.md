# Three way match
An invoice is approved for payment only when it matches the purchase order and the goods receipt on item, quantity and unit price.

# Tolerances
Unit price variance up to 2 percent is accepted automatically. Larger price variances, any quantity billed above quantity received, a missing goods receipt or an unknown purchase order create an exception for accounts payable review.

# Duplicates
An invoice with the same vendor, purchase order and total as an earlier invoice is treated as a possible duplicate even when the invoice number differs.

# Payment
The system never releases payment. It schedules matched invoices for approval by a named person.
