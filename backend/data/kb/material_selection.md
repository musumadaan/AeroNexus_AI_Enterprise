# Aluminum alloys
2024-T3 offers high fatigue resistance and is common in fuselage skins and frames. 7075-T6 has higher strength and is used in wing and landing gear fittings. 6061-T6 is weldable and used for brackets and trays.

# Titanium
TI-6AL-4V is used for engine mounts and high-temperature fittings. It costs several times more than aluminum per kilogram and supplier lead times are long.

# Steel
ASTM A36 carbon steel is used for ground-support equipment and tooling, not flight hardware. Steel sheet may be specified by gauge: 10 gauge is about 3.5 mm.
