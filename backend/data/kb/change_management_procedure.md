# Engineering change request
An engineering change request (ECR) records what changed between drawing revisions, why, and which parts, assemblies, orders and suppliers are affected.

# Engineering change order
An engineering change order (ECO) authorizes the change. It is issued only after the change board approves the impact assessment.

# Impact assessment
Assess cost of re-pricing open orders, scrap of work in progress, obsolete inventory and the lead time of any new material. Changes to material, thickness or critical dimensions on parts already in production are critical.

# Notification
Customers with open orders for the affected part are notified after approval, never before.
