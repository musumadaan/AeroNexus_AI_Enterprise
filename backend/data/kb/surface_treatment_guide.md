# Anodizing
Type II sulfuric acid anodize per MIL-A-8625 applies to aluminum alloys such as 2024-T3, 7075-T6 and 6061-T6. Steel, stainless steel and titanium parts are not Type II anodized.

# Passivation
Passivation per AMS 2700 applies to stainless steels such as SS 304. It removes free iron from the surface and does not change dimensions.

# Priming
Epoxy primer per MIL-PRF-23377 is used on aluminum, steel and titanium structure before topcoat.

# Galvanizing
Hot-dip galvanizing per ASTM A123 applies only to carbon steel such as ASTM A36, typically ground-support equipment. Aerospace aluminum, stainless and titanium parts are never hot-dip galvanized.

# Inconsistent finishes
If a drawing calls for a finish that is not valid for its base material, raise an RFI because the drawing is internally inconsistent.
