# Scope
This internal policy (synthetic, written for the AeroNexus demo) governs how estimators turn customer drawings into quotes.

# Required fields
A quote may only be issued when the drawing number, revision, material, thickness, quantity and coating are confirmed. Missing fields trigger a request for information (RFI) to the customer before pricing.

# Pricing
Unit price equals material cost plus processing cost plus coating markup plus setup cost divided across the quantity. Type II anodize adds roughly 12 percent, priming roughly 15 percent and passivation roughly 8 percent to fabricated part cost.

# Approval
Quotes above 25000 dollars require estimating manager approval. No quote is sent to a customer without a named human approver.

# Validity
Quotes are valid for 30 days because steel prices move with the market.
