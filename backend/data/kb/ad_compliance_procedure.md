# Purpose
This synthetic procedure describes how a maintenance organization tracks airworthiness directives (ADs) against logbook entries. It is decision support only; the final airworthiness determination is always made by an authorized person.

# Recurring directives
A recurring AD is due again at the last compliance time plus the repeat interval. If current time in service exceeds that point, the AD is overdue and the aircraft must not fly until it is complied with.

# Due soon
Treat a recurring AD as due soon when less than 10 percent of the interval remains.

# Evidence
A logbook entry counts as compliance evidence when it names the AD number, or when a qualified reviewer confirms that the entry describes the required action on the required component. Entries that only mention the component need human review.
